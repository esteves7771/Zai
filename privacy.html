Privacy Policy

Zai – Know What You Eat

Last updated: May 2026

🌱 Our Commitment

Zai is built on a simple principle: you have the right to know what you eat and what goes on your skin — without giving up your personal data to do so.

We do not collect personal information. We do not track you. We do not sell data. We do not show ads.

✓ No account required. No personal data collected by Zai.

📱 What We Store on Your Device

All data Zai stores lives exclusively on your device using browser local storage. This includes:

Scan history — the products you have scanned or searched, including product name, brand, image, score and scan date.

Saved products — products you star as favourites.

Allergen preferences — the allergens you select in the allergen setup screen.

App preferences — your selected language, whether you have completed onboarding, and whether you have configured allergens.

✓ None of this data ever leaves your device. Zai cannot access it. It is deleted when you clear your browser data or uninstall the app.

📷 Camera Access

Zai requests access to your camera solely to scan product barcodes. The camera feed is processed in real time on your device to detect barcodes. No images or video are saved, recorded or transmitted at any point.

✓ Camera data never leaves your device.

🌍 Country and Language Detection

To provide more relevant product results for your region, Zai reads your device's browser language setting (for example pt-PT, pt-BR, es-ES, de-DE) and derives a country code from it.

This country code is included as a parameter in API requests to Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts (for example cc=pt for Portugal, cc=br for Brazil). This helps surface products sold in your country first.

We do not store your location or country. We do not use GPS. The country code is derived from your language setting only and is sent as part of each API request.

⚠️ The API servers (Open Food Facts / Open Beauty Facts) receive the country code and barcode as part of standard HTTP requests. See the Third-Party Services section below.

🔍 Third-Party Services

When you scan a barcode or search for a product, Zai sends a request to the following external APIs:

Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) — a free, open-source, non-profit food product database operated by the Open Food Facts association based in France.

Open Beauty Facts (world.openbeautyfacts.org) — a free, open-source, non-profit cosmetic product database operated by the same association.

These requests include the barcode or search query, your derived country code, and standard HTTP headers including your IP address. This is standard web server logging behaviour outside of Zai's control.

Open Food Facts privacy policy: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

⚠️ Zai has no control over how Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts log or process API requests on their servers.

📊 Analytics and Tracking

Zai contains zero analytics, tracking or monitoring tools. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, Sentry, or any equivalent service. We have no visibility into how you use the app.

✓ Nobody is watching. No usage data is collected by Zai.

📢 Advertising

Zai contains no advertising. We do not use advertising networks, advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID), or any form of behavioural profiling.

✓ No ads. Ever.

👶 Children

Zai does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The app contains no features directed at children and collects no personal information from any user regardless of age.
